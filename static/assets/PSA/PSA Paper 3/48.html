Case presentation
An 11-year-old boy is referred to the child and adolescent mental health service with poor concentration, inattention and impulsive behaviour. Weight 36 kg. PMH. Constipation, eczema. DH. Macrogol 3350 with potassium chloride, sodium bicarbonate and sodium chloride (Movicol®) four sachets PO daily, sodium picosulfate 5 mg PO daily, Aveeno® cream TOP 6-hrly.

He is found to have attention deficit hyperactivity disorder and he is to commence treatment with methylphenidate 5 mg PO twice daily, to be increased according to response.

Question
Select the most appropriate option to monitor for adverse effects of this prescription at the start of treatment, and following each dose increase.
